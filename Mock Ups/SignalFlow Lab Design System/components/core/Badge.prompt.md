**Badge** — small rounded-full status pill. Mirrors the product's node-status and lesson-maturity language.

```jsx
<Badge tone="ready">Ready</Badge>
<Badge tone="complete" icon="circle-check">Complete</Badge>
<Badge tone="progress">In progress</Badge>
<Badge tone="locked" icon="lock">Upcoming</Badge>
<Badge tone="context">Context</Badge>
```

Tones: `context` (violet), `ready` (blue), `progress` (amber), `complete` (emerald), `locked`/`neutral` (gray), `info` (indigo).
