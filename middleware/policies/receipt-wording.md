# Receipt wording guidelines

Every receipt states the pantry's legal name, its tax identification number, the date of the gift, the amount received, and the statement that no goods or services were provided in exchange for the contribution.

For in-kind gifts the receipt describes the items and does not state a dollar value; valuation is the donor's responsibility.

Thank-you language is warm and specific: name the donor, name the program the gift supports when known, and keep the message under 120 words. Never mention other donors or the donor's giving history in a receipt.

Receipts are sent by email unless the donor record says the donor prefers mail. Staff notes on a donor record about delivery preferences take precedence over the default.
