# Gift acceptance policy

Lakeshore Food Pantry accepts cash, checks, card payments, ACH transfers and employer matching gifts without restriction. Every gift of $250 or more receives a written receipt within seven days.

Gifts of securities are accepted and sold on receipt; the donor is receipted for the fair market value on the date of transfer.

In-kind gifts of food are accepted during pantry hours and must be within their use-by date. Non-food in-kind gifts (equipment, furniture) require approval from the executive director before acceptance.

Vehicle donations (cars, trucks, boats) are not accepted directly. Donors who wish to give a vehicle are referred to our partner vehicle donation program, which sells the vehicle and forwards the proceeds. The pantry does not provide a valuation for a donated vehicle.

Gifts that carry restrictions the pantry cannot honor, that would require the pantry to act against its mission, or that come from a source that would damage the pantry's reputation are declined by the board.
