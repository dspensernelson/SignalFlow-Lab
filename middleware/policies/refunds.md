# Refund policy

Donations are not refundable as a rule, because they are spent on food purchasing within weeks of receipt.

A refund is issued when a donor made a clear error: a duplicate payment, a wrong amount entered by mistake, or an unauthorized card use. Requests must arrive within 60 days of the gift and go to the finance manager, not to front-desk staff.

Recurring (monthly) gifts can be cancelled at any time by the donor; the cancellation takes effect before the next scheduled charge and no earlier charge is refunded.

Any refund over $500 requires the executive director's written approval and is reported to the board at its next meeting.
