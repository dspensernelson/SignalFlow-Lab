# Module Charters - Index (Modules 2-10)

One ratified charter per remaining module. A charter is the OWNER-level
answer to MODULE_AUTHORING_PLAYBOOK.md Step 1 (scenario) and the spine for
Step 2 (map): the org, the deliverable, the FIXED role list, the signature
concept cluster, a draft node spine with the module's one fork and one
temporal loop, tier postures, and engine needs.

RATIFICATION: all nine charters were ratified 2026-07-02 by standing owner
approval ("let it loose and it builds everything" - see DECISION_LOG.md,
2026-07-02 autonomy pass). A builder executes them without further scenario
approval, under AUTONOMY_CHARTER.md. What a builder may and may not change
inside a ratified charter is defined there (short version: refine node
details at the map step, never change org, roles, deliverable, or signature
cluster).

Build order is fixed: modules ship one at a time, in numeric order, each as
its own PR with the module report (playbook Step 8).

Every module keeps the Meridian invariants on its map (an intake record, at
least one governed policy/reference, a cleaning/normalization step, exactly
one decision fork, human handoffs with captured responses, one assembly
output, one archive closing a temporal loop) so the recurring shape itself
stays the deepest lesson. Keep ASCII-only.

Every module also ships, without exception:

- A MANDATORY Hard-tier solo-rebuild capstone (MODULE_AUTHORING_PLAYBOOK.md
  Step 7a): an artifactImport lesson on the decision-fork node that has the
  learner rebuild the module's four spine artifacts outside the app, each
  graded by its original easy-tier validator.
- A SPEND-OR-JUSTIFY interaction budget (Step 7b): spend all four exercise
  types across the tiers, or record in DECISION_LOG.md why one is absent.
